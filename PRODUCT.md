# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Music producers learning the Softube catalog. Readers may already own some plug-ins or may be researching a possible purchase, but the guide does not sell products or steer readers toward buying.

## Product Purpose

The Softube Field Guide is an independent research and information tool that explains the complete Softube catalog in practical production terms. It helps producers understand what each product does, what it models or draws from, when it is useful, and how closely related offerings differ.

Success means a producer can quickly orient themselves, narrow the catalog by production job, compare plausible choices, and then read deeper without having to scan every entry.

## Positioning

The guide combines complete catalog coverage with candid hardware-lineage research, practical selection guidance, and production-specific techniques. It is neither a storefront nor an official Softube manual.

## Operating Context

Readers consult the guide while learning the ecosystem, comparing plug-ins they already own, deciding which tool fits a mix or mastering task, or researching unfamiliar products. Mixing and mastering workflows are the primary path through the catalog; synthesizers, modular, guitar, bass, suites, and compatibility products remain available as secondary areas.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve coverage of the whole catalog.
- Prioritize mixing and mastering plug-ins in hierarchy and wayfinding.
- Keep search and deep-link navigation.
- Preserve the distinction between official models, unlicensed identifications, archetypes or lineages, Softube originals, and legacy compatibility products.
- Keep the site deployable as a static GitHub Page.
- Do not introduce sales language, affiliate behavior, prices, purchase calls to action, or invented claims.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named “The Softube Field Guide.” It must be prominently and repeatedly identified as an independent editorial reference, not an official Softube publication. Its interface should nevertheless feel instantly familiar to people in the Softube ecosystem through disciplined use of the brand’s recognizable visual vocabulary, without copying official trade dress or implying endorsement.

The visual direction is the category standard played straight and executed at a high craft level: familiar documentation and catalog conventions should maximize comprehension instead of introducing a novel interface metaphor. Softube’s current catalog sets the ecosystem-familiarity bar, FabFilter sets the product-clarity bar, and iZotope’s learning library sets the educational-structure bar.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing `index.html` contains the complete researched guide: 166 named plug-ins, suite components, and compatibility entries; 12 genre recipes; hardware-lineage notes; research sources; and a roster verification date of 24 August 2026. No official Softube brand asset license, endorsement, testimonial, or partnership evidence is present and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Research before promotion: explain rather than sell.
- Choose by job before flavor: make production intent the main way into the catalog.
- Complete, but progressively disclosed: retain the full catalog without presenting it all at once.
- Mixing and mastering first: give the primary use cases the strongest hierarchy while keeping every catalog area reachable.
- Familiar but independent: echo the ecosystem’s craft and clarity while making editorial independence unmistakable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The guide should remain keyboard navigable, readable at mobile and desktop sizes, usable with reduced motion, and structured with semantic headings, landmarks, labels, and visible focus states.
